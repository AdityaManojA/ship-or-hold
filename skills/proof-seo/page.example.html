Pocket notebooks made to last

A Larkspur notebook is a small thing done carefully. Each one is forty-eight pages of heavy paper, folded once, stitched through the spine with waxed thread, and trimmed by hand. It fits a back pocket, survives a season of being sat on, and takes a fountain pen without feathering or bleeding through to the other side. We make them in runs of a few hundred at a time in a workshop behind a bakery, which is why the first batch of every year smells faintly of bread.

Why the paper matters

Most pocket notebooks use paper around 70 gsm because it is cheap and folds flat. It also lets ink soak through, so one side of every sheet is wasted. We use 100 gsm paper with a light sizing, which is the treatment that keeps ink sitting on the surface long enough to dry crisp. Pencil grips it well, gel pens do not smear, and a broad fountain pen nib leaves a clean line with a little shading. We tested nineteen papers before settling on this one, and we publish the results on our paper page so you can check our work.

How a notebook is made

Sheets are cut to size, folded in signatures of six, and nested together. The cover is a 300 gsm card stock that is scored before folding so the spine does not crack. Then the whole block is stitched, not stapled, because staples rust and tear out. A stapled notebook that gets wet is finished; a stitched one dries and carries on.

Stitching

We use a three-hole pamphlet stitch with a linen thread that is waxed by hand. It takes about ninety seconds per notebook. The knot sits inside the spine where it cannot catch on a pocket lining, and the thread ends are trimmed short and pressed flat.

Sizes and prices

The pocket size is 90 by 140 millimetres and comes in a pack of three for eighteen dollars. The larger field size is 120 by 190 millimetres and comes in a pack of two for twenty dollars. Both are available ruled, dot grid or plain. Shipping is flat rate and we ship the notebooks flat in a board mailer so they arrive without a bent corner.

Questions people ask

Will the paper take fountain pen ink?

Yes. That was the point of the whole project. Very wet nibs with very wet inks may show faintly on the reverse, but nothing bleeds through, and the paper page shows photos of twelve common inks on it.

What if the stitching comes loose?

Send it back within a year and we replace it, no questions asked. In four years it has happened twice, and both times the thread had been cut by a key in the same pocket.

Do you make custom covers?

For orders of fifty or more we print a one-colour mark on the cover. Write to us through the contact page with the quantity and the artwork and we will quote within two working days.
